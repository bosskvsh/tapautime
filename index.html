TapauTime Merchant FAQ

Clear answers on how TapauTime works, zero-cost operations, hardware requirements, and automated payouts.

1 What is TapauTime?
expand_more
TapauTime is a digital ordering and kitchen management platform built specifically for independent F&B stalls and kopitiams. We provide you with table QR ordering, a Kitchen Display System (KDS), and automated payouts, all without the fees of traditional delivery apps.
2 How much does TapauTime cost?
expand_more
Zero setup fees, zero hardware costs, and zero monthly subscriptions. We charge a flat 5% fee on completed orders to customers. You keep 100% of your sales.
3 Do my customers need to download an app?
expand_more
No app download is required. Your customers simply scan the physical QR code on their table (or click your store link for takeaway), browse your menu, and place their order directly through their phone's web browser.
4 Do I need to buy expensive POS hardware?
expand_more
Not at all. TapauTime is entirely cloud-based. Your Kitchen Display System (KDS) and merchant dashboard run perfectly on any standard tablet, smartphone, or laptop you already own.
5 How do I get paid?
expand_more
Your payouts are 100% automated. All digital payments collected from your customers are batched and settled directly to your registered Malaysian bank account. It takes 5-7 business working days for your payouts to be settled.
6 What payment methods can my customers use?
expand_more
We only accept Online Banking (FPX), and E-Wallets only. No cash.
7 It gets incredibly loud in my kitchen. Will I miss orders?
expand_more
No. We built the TapauTime KDS specifically for noisy kopitiam environments. The system features a loud, built-in audio chime that alerts you the second a new ticket drops in, so you can stay focused on the wok.
8 Do I need to register for SST to use this?
expand_more
For the vast majority of hawkers, no. In Malaysia, F&B operators only need to register for Sales and Service Tax (SST) if their stall's revenue exceeds RM 1.5 million a year. Unless you hit that threshold, the food you sell remains SST-free for your customers.
9 Is TapauTime available everywhere in Malaysia?
expand_more
Unfortunately, no. At this moment, TapauTime is only available in East Malaysia.
10 How long does it take to get my stall online?
expand_more
Exactly 5 minutes to fill out the onboarding application form. Once submitted, our team will review your application. It will take 2-3 business working days to review and approve your application.
